You don't have to wait for anyone: grassroots democracy begins with you, from the bottom up. People around the country are starting WhatsApp and Facebook group chats, arranging small meetings of friends, neighbours, and planning larger community assemblies.

These are guides to help you build Your Party's local branch so that it's...
- Maximally democratic
- In solidarity with all oppressed groups
- Easy for members to get involved in collective action and struggle, to build people power against capitalism and the state.
