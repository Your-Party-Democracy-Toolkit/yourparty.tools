Ahead of a formal organisation, there's a need for interim democratic structures that are good enough for now, safe enough to try, and help us get everyone involved and active.

There are different approaches to this:
- Elected organising committee with defined roles
- Elected organising Committee -> working groups report back to organising committee
- All-members' meeting assembly -> multiple working groups in the interim

### Structures for different activities

The way you structure your branch should reflect the kind of activities you collectively want to be involved in.

Your Party is a place for communities and campaigners of all stripes to come together to build power. That could be:
- Working groups that organise in workplaces through [rank-and-file organising](https://rfs.dsausa.org/introduction/) or trade union campaigning
- Working groups that focus in social movement organising like setting up an [Apartheid Free Zone](https://www.bdsmovement.net/apartheid-free-zones), an [Anti-Raids Group](https://files.libcom.org/files/2022-06/anti-raids-zine.pdf), or a [tenants union](https://england.shelter.org.uk/housing_advice/eviction/how_renters_unions_can_help)
- Working groups that collect grievances on the doorstep, via stalls or at assemblies so that we can build campaigns that help them out
