# Visual guide to the Your Party founding documents

::div{class="text-muted-foreground pt-1 text-lg"}
  This is a visual representation of the party constitution proposal that Jeremy Corbyn's leadership team has proposed to the membership.
::

- Your Party has [released](https://www.yourparty.uk/founding-documents/) four founding documents for consultation: [Constitution](https://www.yourparty.uk/constitution/), [Standing Orders](https://www.yourparty.uk/standing-orders/), [Political Statement](https://www.yourparty.uk/political-statement/) and  [Organisational Strategy](https://www.yourparty.uk/organisational-strategy/)
- They are running a [consultation process](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1fW-Lpbyqn7UTAF3iJD8CFVUMv7RlMcsx7XS9yKTAsbY/edit?tab=t.0#) which involves members' opinions being collected via ["regional assembly" consultation meetings](https://www.yourparty.uk/assemblies/) between now and founding conference on Sat 9-30 November.
- This diagram is meant to be a helpful visual representation of their proposal, to help members assess it and develop opinions, proposals and suggested amendments. To that end, please [send us feedback](/suggest-an-improvement) if you notice an inaccuracy, omission or legibility issue.

::alert{type="warning"}
  - Please note: We are not Your Party! [We are a third party group of activists](/about). Do not send us your opinions about the conference or the constitution.
  - To organise for your opinions to be heard by the party, we recommend learning more about the [Unity Platform group of political factions](https://prometheusjournal.org/2025/10/13/for-a-member-led-socialist-party/) who are organising for members' voices to be effectively expressed at the regional assemblies and founding conference.
::
