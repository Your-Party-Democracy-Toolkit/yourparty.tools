### About us

This toolkit was started by people who are members of [rs21](https://revsoc21.uk/), the [Democratic Socialist caucus](https://linktr.ee/DemSocsYP) and the [Party Republic caucus](https://medium.com/@maxshanly/drafting-the-future-adbce6f51aa4), and includes contributions from many more people. But the toolkit is for all members of Your Party. This is a non-partisan project. We accept contributions from any and all, in service of a democratic, liberatory, struggle-oriented party. If you want to get involved, [email info@yourparty.tools](mailto\:info@yourparty.tools).
