### In the run up to conference

Your Party members will potentially be involved in regional assemblies and in the national conference, either creating, reviewing or voting on proposals such as the national constitution, key principles and other big decisions.

Political discussions and debates at a local level are important, then, to help members to develop their thinking on how local issues influence [key debates](/info/key-debates) playing out nationally.

### In preparation for a long-term branch operations

People are motivated by different priorities and ideas. It's important to allow this to flourish, while developing a collective consciousness. Discussion could include topics like:

- policy and programme
- local grievances and needs
- values and principles

::alert{type="example" icon="lucide:test-tube"}
In Leicester, a large assembly gathered people in small breakout groups to discuss three questions, before bringing them back to the main assembly to discuss the findings:
1. What issue most important to you personally, for Your Party to deal with?
2. What about locally?
3. What about nationally?
::

But collective conciousness is also developed through relationship-building, [collective action](/organising/principles/collective-action) and [big gatherings](/organising/activities/big-gatherings).
