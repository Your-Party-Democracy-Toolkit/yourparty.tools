Welcome to **yourparty.tools** — a practical toolkit to help you build [Your Party](https://www.yourparty.uk/) and instil a culture of [democracy](/organising/principles/democracy), [solidarity](/organising/principles/solidarity) and [collective action](/organising/principles/collective-action).

You don't have to wait for anyone: grassroots democracy begins with you, from the bottom up. People around the country are [organising proto-branches](/organising/branch), setting up WhatsApp and Facebook [group chats](/organising/infrastructure/group-chats), arranging small [meetings](/organising/activities/meetings) of friends, neighbours, and planning larger [community assemblies](/organising/activities/big-gatherings).

The best place to start is with the [branch guide](/organising/branch).

### In case you missed it...

- In the Summer of 2025, Your Party was announced by Zarah Sultana and Jeremy Corbyn, two ex-Labour MPs.
- Since then, around 800,000 people have signed up to join the party.
- As at 29 August, [at least 54 grassroots-led "proto-branches"](/find-your-local) have started gathering.
- At some point later in 2025 or 2026, there will be some kind of founding conference. This will be a chance for all the proto-branches to come together and agree on the party's principles, programme, and policies.
- Some people in Your Party are interested in running election campaigns in May 2026. In England, this will be for the local elections, and in Scotland, this will be for the Holyrood parliamentary elections.

### About us

This toolkit was started by people who are members of [rs21](https://revsoc21.uk/), the [Democratic Socialist caucus](https://linktr.ee/DemSocsYP) and the [Party Republic caucus](https://medium.com/@maxshanly/drafting-the-future-adbce6f51aa4). But the toolkit is for all members of Your Party. This is a non-partisan project. We accept contributions from any and all, in service of a democratic, liberatory, struggle-oriented party. If you want to get involved, [email info@yourparty.tools](mailto\:info@yourparty.tools).
