---
title: Joining an undemocratic group
description: How things start doesn't have to determine how they end.
updated: 2025-08-22
---

Practicing democracy day to day will ingrain into the culture of the party.

One way of democratising your group is to adopt a [constitution](/organising/guides/branch-constitution).

If your group has a self-appointed organising committee, take a look at alternative [branch structures](</organising/guides/Setting up a proto-branch/structure>) and ways you can get there.
