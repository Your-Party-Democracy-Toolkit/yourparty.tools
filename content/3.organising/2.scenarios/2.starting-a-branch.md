title: Starting a proto-branch
description: How to get started the best way possible
updated: 2025-08-22

This toolkit is a collaborative resource to provide guidance for activists in new groups to bring together comrades in your area as proto-branches for ‘Your Party’. Existing local activist groups may also find this helpful.

This toolkit is written as a ‘best guess’ on key issues that are likely to come up as we approach the founding conference so that comrades feel well prepared and informed, assuming:

- ‘Your Party’ is currently in its early stages, primarily operating with an email sign-up list only.
- A founding conference for the party is anticipated in several months, with rumours suggesting this could be as soon as November 2025.
- Until the conference, no formal structures, processes, membership, or rules are in place.
- A 'proto-branch' refers to bringing comrades together in preparation for a new left party, to engage in debate, organise activists, and lay the groundwork for a local branch.
