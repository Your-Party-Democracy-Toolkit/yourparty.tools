Practicing democracy day to day will ingrain into the culture of the party.

### Identify the situation in your area

::card-group
  ::card
    **Form a completely new YP Proto-Branch.**
    Focus on establishing democratic structures and wide engagement with activists in your area.
  ::

  ::card
    **Form a YP Proto-Branch with existing political and/or community groups.**
    Develop tactics for working together to bring about a democratic structure that accountable to YP members, not to third parties.
  ::

  ::card
    **Form a Proto-Branch as a caucus or faction.**
    Developing democratic structures to organise a caucus or as a faction.
  ::
::

One way of democratising your group is to adopt a [constitution](/organising/guides/branch-constitution).

If your group has a self-appointed organising committee, take a look at alternative [branch structures](/organising/guides/Setting%20up%20a%20proto-branch/structure) and ways you can get there.

### Make decisions before there's a constitution

Short term committees that dissolve, meetings are sovereign
