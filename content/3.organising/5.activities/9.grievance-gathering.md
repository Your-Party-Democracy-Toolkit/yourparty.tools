Many socialist parties around the world have used the tactic of "grievance collection" to build relationships and learn how to act together.

There are many ways to collect grievances, and many ways to act on them, such as:
- Doorknocking to ask people about key issues
- Street stalls and surveys
- [Public assemblies](/organising/activities/big-gatherings)
- Online surveys

Grievance collection builds power when it is coupled with [collective action](/organising/principles/collective-action).
