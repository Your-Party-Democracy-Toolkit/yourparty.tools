::alert{type="info"}
  This page discusses good practice for larger meetings such as for assemblies or conferences. For smaller meetings [see this page](/organising/scenarios/a-small-meeting). 
::

Anyone can organise a public gathering under the banner of Your Party — after all, it's *your* party. You might organise one for...
- People in the same neighbourhood or community.
- Parents and teachers in your local schools.
- People in the same industry or profession.

For example, are you mostly hearing from guest speakers with questions? An online only meeting may suffice. Is there going to be a vote? You might need extra volunteers as tellers if your meeting is in person only.

If you need guidance, refer to the Proto Branch Toolkit that sets out:

suggested meeting aims, debates and topics to include in your meetings
branch committee structures to help you establish decision making processes
