Your Party meetings and activities are successful if members are actively involved in them.

### Elections are part of a bigger picture

Elections are a way to build power, but they are not the only way. Election campaigns suck up resources, energy and enthusiasm and look like collective action, but they do not build power so much as they spend it in the hope of winning access to state power.

And winning elections is not a guarantee of success: politicians are not always accountable to the people who elected them, and the state is not always a force for good — for example when it is used to suppress working class power.

If elections are won, they can help us build power by transferring money, resources and assets back to local control for [democratic](/organising/principles/democracy) deliberation.

But members' time and energy can also be used to build power "from below" — for example, by building trusting relationships in workplaces, neighbourhoods and communities — and by building our own well-resourced organisations like trade unions, tenants unions, community kitchens and so on.

### Branch structures can help members get active

Structures like [member sections](organising/guides/structure#member-sections-fractions) can help members get active in different ways:
- Working groups that organise in workplaces through [rank-and-file organising](https://rfs.dsausa.org/introduction/) or trade union campaigning
- Working groups that focus in social movement organising like setting up an [Apartheid Free Zone](https://www.bdsmovement.net/apartheid-free-zones), an [Anti-Raids Group](https://files.libcom.org/files/2022-06/anti-raids-zine.pdf), or a [tenants union](https://england.shelter.org.uk/housing_advice/eviction/how_renters_unions_can_help)
- Working groups that collect grievances on the doorstep, via stalls or at assemblies so that we can build campaigns that help them out

Focusing branch meetings on collective action, rather than just administration or election campaigning, can also keep members engaged and active.
